Community skins

Skins shared through MouZe's open catalog, an alternative to the Steam Workshop. Subscribe in the app under Discover, or download a skin and drop it on the MouZe window. Anyone can host a catalog; here's how.

What's in the app

Native, safe, fast

Cursors are applied the way Mouse Properties does it on Windows and as an Xcursor theme on Linux. Nothing touches other processes, so it's fine next to anti-cheat, and idle CPU use is zero.

Effects

Ribbon, comet, sparkle and ghost trails, click ripples and confetti, a highlight halo, a presentation spotlight and shake-to-find.

Studio

Design a full cursor set from a shape, style and colours, or build one from your own PNG, GIF, SVG, CUR or ANI art with a click-to-set hotspot editor.

Bring your cursors

Imports Mouse X Workshop skins, Windows .cur/.ani packs, Xcursor and hyprcursor themes, Mousecape .cape files and CSS cursor packs.

Profiles & hotkeys

Switch skin, size and effects per app or game, or by time of day. Global hotkeys include a panic restore back to your original cursors.
